Glossary

FVG (Fair Value Gap): A price gap where one candle's range doesn't overlap the next, indicating imbalance. Bullish FVG = gap upward, Bearish FVG = gap downward.

OB (Order Block): The last candle before a strong price move — marks where institutions likely placed large orders. Bullish OB = demand zone, Bearish OB = supply zone.

Inflexion: A swing high or swing low pivot point. Bull (concave) = resistance at swing high, Bear (convex) = support at swing low.

Mitigation = price returns to touch/fill a zone. Respected = zone held. Disrespected = price broke through.

Glossary

MFE (Max Favorable Excursion): After a zone is touched (mitigated), how far did price move in the zone's expected direction before the zone was broken?

Reference price: For FVG/OB — the high (bullish) or low (bearish) of the mitigation candle. For Inflexions — the level itself.

Categories:

  • Direct disrespect: Broken on the same candle it was touched. No reversal opportunity.
  • No reversal, then disrespect: Had a window between touch and break, but price never moved in the expected direction.
  • Reversal then disrespect: Price did reverse (MFE > 0) but eventually broke through anyway.
  • Respected (held): Zone was touched and never broken. MFE measured to end of data.
  • Pending: Zone was never touched.
